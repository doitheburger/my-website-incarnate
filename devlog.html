9/2/2026 dungeoneerlog 4: i *dropped* my logic!

i made it so items spawn in rooms. thats cool. right?...
and i also had an issue with dropping that you can probably see in the gif somewhere above me
so whenever you dropped an item, and theres an item you've already dropped then the other item you've dropped teleported to the new one
ex: you have 2 cans of spray paint, you drop spraypaint 1. you move to the side, and you drop spray paint 2. spray paint 1 then teleports to where you dropped spray paint 2
but if you have a rock and a can of spraypaint and you drop the rock, move, and then drop the rock, they would stay in the same place.
after like a trillion hours of me trying to figure out what the problem was, i found it...
the issue was that when the game started, it added a tool to one of your hands directly from the table full of the tools
the issue here is that i thought when you did that, it created a clone of the tools into your hand, but instead it acted more like a path to the original
so when you dropped that item it changed the position of the ORIGINAL item from the table to the player
and since the other item is pulling from the same global item, it changes the position of the global value to the new item you dropped, also moving the other item
so the way i fixed this is instead of you starting with items, it spawns clones of the pulled item which can all have their own individual variables.
haaha! this took me like 5 hours to figure out.
